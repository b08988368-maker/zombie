# HOLLOWFIELD — Easter Egg Guide ("Blackout Broadcast")

## Overview
Deep inside Hollowfield Agricultural Research Station, the emergency telemetry transmitter in the Lab Wing can be restored to broadcast the final containment report.

## Step 1: Turn On Station Power
- Open the doors from **Farmhouse** → **Silo Yard** (750 pts) → **Lab Wing** (1250 pts).
- Flip the **Power Switch** on the east wall of the Lab Wing.

## Step 2: Locate & Collect the 3 Blown Fuses
Once power is restored, three sparking high-voltage fuse cartridges can be found and picked up with `F`:
1. **Farmhouse Fuse**: On the kitchen counter beside the rear loop doorway.
2. **Barn Fuse**: Upstairs on the hayloft railing overlooking the Silo Yard.
3. **Bunker Fuse**: Inside the Underground Bunker next to the Overclock Bench (requires opening the Bunker door for 1500 pts).

## Step 3: Restore & Defend the Emergency Radio
- Bring all 3 fuses to the **Station Radio Console** in the **Lab Wing** and hold `F` to insert them and start the transmission.
- Survive a 40-second emergency defense wave while aggressive infected surge toward the Lab Wing.
- **Reward:** When the transmission completes, you receive **+5000 Points**, a **Free Max Ammo**, and a unique station radio sign-off broadcast.
